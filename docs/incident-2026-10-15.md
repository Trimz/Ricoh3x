# Incident 2026-10-15: bulk settings write left the camera freezing after every shot

**Read this before writing more than a handful of settings.**

## What was done

Test 9: `tools/grsettings.py profile` turned a settings export into 956 `Item = Value` lines
(the camera's *current* values, deny-listed items removed), and `grsettings.py script` wrote
them all back with `UserData Set` in one `startup.ttl`, plus a `dd_out` export at power-off.
The intent was a no-op round-trip to prove backup/restore.

## What happened

- The script completed (it ran on two boots).
- Afterwards: very slow menus, noise in live view, then **the camera froze after every shot**.
- The power-off export differed from the pre-test backup in the live settings struct and in all
  user-mode copies. So writing back "the same values" was **not** a no-op: decoded values
  do not always round-trip through the shell parsers and setters.

## Recovery attempts (none fixed the freeze)

1. Setup menu → Reset Settings: menus fast again, but shooting still freezes after a shot.
2. Card formatted in camera.
3. Official firmware 1.60 reinstalled (MENU + power).
4. Developer flags reverted: `[DBG_LOG_STORE_EN_MODE DIS]`, `[SCRIPT_EN_MODE DIS]`
   (the camera confirmed "script disable complete").

The camera went to Ricoh service with a written description of everything above.

## Rules that follow

- **Never bulk-write settings.** Write only the few items you need, each one from the
  verified list or tested on its own first. `grsettings.py script` now refuses profiles with
  more than 20 items.
- "Restore from export" is **not safe**. Decoded export values are not guaranteed to be
  valid input for `UserData Set`.
- The settings that Reset Settings restores do not cover everything the bulk write touched.
  Some damage survives a reset and a firmware reinstall.
- Earlier small tests (up to 10 items, set and restored, verified by export) did not cause
  problems. The risk scales with how many and which items you touch.
